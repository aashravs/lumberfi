## 3. USERS

The `Users` sheet acts as a simple Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) datastore, read by `getCurrentUser()`:
- `Email` (Column A)
- `Name` (Column B)
- `Role` (Column C) - Valid values: Admin, Manager, AE
- `Manager Email` (Column D)
- `IsActive` (Column E)

**RBAC Mapping:**
- **Admin**: Has full access. Can view all data and trigger recalculations (`adminRecalculate`) and uploads (`adminUploadDeals`).
- **Manager**: Can view calculations and payouts only for users whose `Manager Email` matches their own email.
- **AE**: Can view calculations and payouts only where the `Deal Owner` matches their `Name`.

## 4. BUSINESS ASSUMPTIONS

The business rules hardcoded in `Code.gs` (and likely documented in the `Assumption` sheet) are:

- **Plan Period**: Q3 2026 (1 July 2026 to 30 September 2026). Deals closing outside this window are ineligible (`inPlan = false`).
- **ARR Calculation**: `ARR = TCV - Implementation Fee`.
- **Channel Determination**: `Lead Source === "Partnership"` -> Partner, else Direct.
- **ARR Commission Rates**:
  - Direct: 10%
  - Partner: 5%
- **Implementation Commission Rates**:
  - Based on `Implementation Fee / TCV`:
    - `<= 10%`: 0% commission.
    - `11% - 20%`: Direct (10%), Partner (5%).
    - `> 20%`: Direct (15%), Partner (10%).
- **Advance Payout**: 25% of ARR Commission paid on the Monday following the 10th of the month after Close Date.
- **Implementation Payout**:
  - "100% at signing": 100% paid on Monday following 15th of the month after Close Date.
  - "50% at signing / 50% at go-live": 50% paid as above; 50% paid on Monday following 15th of the month after Go-Live Collection Date.
- **Balance / Recurring Payouts**:
  - **Annual**: Remaining 75% paid on the Last Friday of the month following Collection.
  - **Monthly/Quarterly**: Remaining 75% recovered proportionately per invoice; net amount paid on the Last Friday of the month following Collection, capped at 31-Mar-2027.
- **Clawbacks**: If cancelled before Go-Live, 100% of Advance and Implementation commissions paid prior to cancellation are clawed back on the Monday following the 10th of the month after cancellation.

## 5. CALCULATION PIPELINE

The legacy pipeline executes linearly:
1. `getDeals()`: Extracts raw deals.
2. `normalizeDealRows()` / `parseMoneyValue()`: Cleans inputs on upload.
3. `calculateFinancials()`: Derives `inPlan`, `arr`, `channel`, and flat commission rates.
4. `runFinancialCalculations()`: Writes derived financials to `Calculations` sheet.
5. `createAnnualPayouts()` & `createRecurringPayouts()`: Generates payout events.
6. `createCancellationClawback()`: Generates negative payouts if cancelled early.
7. `runPayoutCalculations()`: Writes combined events to `Payouts` sheet.

## 8. PAYOUTS SHEET

Produced by `runPayoutCalculations()`.

Columns: Deal ID, Deal Owner, Component, Invoice Date, Collection Date, Payout Date, Gross Commission, Recovered Advance, Payout Amount.

**Component Types Discovered:**
- `Advance`: 25% upfront.
- `Implementation`: 100% if single payment.
- `Implementation - Signing`: 1st half of split payment.
- `Implementation - Go-Live`: 2nd half of split payment.
- `Balance`: Remaining 75% for Annual Billing.
- `Monthly Commission` / `Quarterly Commission`: Amortized payout net of advance recovery.
- `Cancellation Clawback`: Negative payout recovering previous commissions.
